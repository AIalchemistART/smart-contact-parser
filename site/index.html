Built on Principles

Smart Contact Parser isn't just a tool — it's a philosophy about how your data should be handled.

🔑

Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Use your own OpenAI API key. It's stored in your browser's localStorage only — never sent to our servers, never logged, never shared. You control your AI costs and your credentials.

🛡️

Zero Server Storage

Contact data is processed entirely client-side via direct OpenAI API calls from your browser. No database on our end, no server-side processing, no data retention. When you close the tab, it's gone.

🌐

Open Source & Transparent

MIT Licensed. Every line of code is auditable on GitHub. No hidden analytics, no tracking pixels, no telemetry. You can verify exactly what happens with your data.

💜

Community-Sustained

Free forever, sustained by community donations. No investors demanding monetization of your data. The incentives are aligned — building the best tool possible for people who need it.

🔐 Why Client-Side Processing Matters

Contact data is some of the most sensitive business information you handle. Names, emails, phone numbers, and company affiliations are high-value targets for data breaches. Smart Contact Parser sends your data directly from your browser to OpenAI's API — our servers never see it, never store it, never touch it. Combined with BYOK, this is the most private AI contact parsing architecture possible.

Powerful Features

Everything you need to turn chaos into clean, structured contacts

🤖

AI Smart Paste

Paste messy, unstructured text and let GPT-4o extract structured contact fields. Handles freeform notes, mixed formats, and incomplete data gracefully.

📷

Vision Support

Upload photos of business cards, handwritten notes, or scanned documents. GPT-4o's vision capabilities extract contacts from images just as easily as text.

📚

Batch Mode

Process large Word documents in chunks. Paste 10-20 pages at a time and contacts accumulate in the results panel. Export everything at once when done.

🎯

Confidence Scores

See how certain the AI is about each extracted field. Low confidence? Review and correct before export. High confidence? Trust and move on.

📏

Parsing Rules

Create persistent rules that train the AI on your preferences. Set default tags, sources, field mappings, and special handling instructions that apply to every parse.

📊

GHL-Ready CSV Export

Export directly to GoHighLevel-compatible CSV format. Proper column mapping, pipe-separated notes with date prefixes, and tag handling out of the box.
